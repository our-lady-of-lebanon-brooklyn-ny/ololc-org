Awards for Church &amp; Community Service are presented to deserving candidates whose commitment reflects the mission and goals of the Christian faith.
Grants will be given to *Elementary School* graduates attending High School and *High School* graduates going to College who are actively involved in the Church activity.
An independent Scholarship Committee will determine the strongest and worthiest applicants based on their fulfillment of all requirements.

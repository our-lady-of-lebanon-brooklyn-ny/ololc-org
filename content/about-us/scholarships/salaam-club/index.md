This scholarship, administered through the Salaam Club Foundation, empowers talented undergraduate students of Middle Eastern heritage
to pursue higher education and contribute positively to their communities.
The Award: $10,000 one-time grant for tuition, books, educational expenses, or related costs during the upcoming academic year.

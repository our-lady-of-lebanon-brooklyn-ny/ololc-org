Awards for Church & Community Service are presented by the Confraternity to deserving candidates whose commitment reflects the mission and goals of the Christian faith.  
Grant of $500.00 will be awarded to Elementary School graduates attending Catholic High School who are actively involved in the Church activity.  
The deadline for submission is June 2, 2026.

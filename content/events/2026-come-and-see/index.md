Is Jesus calling you to Come and See? Are you seeking to grow in your ability to hear God’s voice and to learn more about prayer and the gift of religious life? 

The Maronite Servants of Christ the Light located in Dartmouth MA, invite single young adult women (ages 18-39) to prayerfully consider joining them for a Come & See Retreat Weekend, November 13-15, 2026.

The weekend is designed to give young adult women a glimpse into the prayer and communal life of the Sisters, and to enjoy fellowship with other young adult women discerning and seeking to go deeper in their relationship with Christ our Light.

If you have questions or are interested in exploring the gift of religious life, please contact Vocation Director, Sister Therese Maria, vocations@maroniteservants.org. Single young adult women interested in registering can scan the QR Code or sign up at this link: https://forms.gle/KwvwGJUnXLt3kPRa6

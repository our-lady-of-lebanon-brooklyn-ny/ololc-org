The parish family of *Our Lady of Lebanon Maronite Cathedral*
cordially invites you to join them for the ordination of
**Very Reverend Dominique Hanna** to the Order of Periodeut with the title *Monsignor*.
Through the laying on of hands by the **Most Reverend Gregory J. Mansour**, *Bishop of the Eparchy of St. Maron of Brooklyn*.
With the blessing of the *Maronite Patriarch* **His Beatitude Bechara Peter Cardinal Rai**.

Sunday, April 12, 2026 at 11:30 {{< am >}} at the Cathedral.

Banquet to follow in the Church Hall

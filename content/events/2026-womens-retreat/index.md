Dear Brother Priests, Deacons/Subdeacons, Consecrated Men & Women, Lay Faithful,


Please find attached a flyer introducing the [2nd annual Women’s Retreat](https://saintsharbelcenter.org/retreats/womens-retreat-with-johnnette-benkovic-williams-of-ewtn) in person at our Saint Sharbel Spiritual Life Center in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Johnette Benkovic Williams presents a biblical understanding of women’s gifts, based on solid Church teaching.
